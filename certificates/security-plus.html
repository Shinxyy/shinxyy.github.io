About Security+ Certification

CompTIA Security+ is a globally trusted certification that validates the baseline skills necessary to perform core security functions and pursue an IT security career. As one of the most widely recognized cybersecurity certifications, Security+ demonstrates competency in essential security topics and hands-on skills with the latest techniques and tools used by security professionals worldwide.

Industry Standards: Security+ is approved by the U.S. Department of Defense (DoD) as one of the required certifications for Information Assurance (IA) positions and meets ISO 17024 standards.
