HALON-2023 - Top Performer Recognition Award

HALON Competition Overview

HALON is a security research competition that challenges participants to identify vulnerabilities across multiple participating Dutch Universities. The competition emphasizes real-world security research skills and responsible disclosure practices.

Recognition Achievement

This award recognizes not just the quantity of vulnerabilities found, but the breadth and scope of security research conducted across multiple organizations. It demonstrates comprehensive security assessment capabilities and consistent performance across diverse environments and systems.

Impact on Security Research

By identifying vulnerabilities across multiple organizations, this achievement contributed to improving the overall security posture of participating entities. The research helped organizations understand and remediate critical security weaknesses in their infrastructure.
